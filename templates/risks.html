Common Risks and Threats

1. Oversharing Personal Information

Sharing details like home address, daily routine, travel plans, or school/workplace information can expose users to stalking, burglary, or targeted scams.

2. Phishing and Social Engineering

Fraudulent messages or links trick users into revealing sensitive information such as passwords or bank details, often by exploiting trust, fear, or urgency.

3. Cyberbullying and Harassment

Repeated online harassment, threats, or humiliation can cause serious emotional and psychological harm, particularly to minors.

4. Identity Theft and Doxxing

Identity theft misuses someone's personal information to impersonate them or commit fraud. Doxxing maliciously publishes private information without consent.

5. Fake Profiles and Scams

Scammers create fake profiles with minimal history, generic photos, and urgent requests for money or information.

6. Unsecured Networks and Weak Passwords

Using public Wi-Fi without protection or relying on weak, reused passwords significantly increases the risk of account compromise.
