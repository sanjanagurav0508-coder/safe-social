About the Project

Social media has become an important part of everyday life. People use social platforms for communication, education, entertainment, networking and sharing information. However, careless online behaviour can expose users to privacy and security risks.


This project focuses on creating awareness about social media safety and digital footprints. It helps users understand how posts, photos, comments, likes, profiles, locations and other online interactions contribute to their digital footprint.


The project combines field-study research with an interactive awareness platform. Users can learn about online risks, understand safer digital practices and think about the possible long-term impact of what they share online.

What We Focus On

Important areas of social media safety and digital awareness

🔐

Privacy Settings

Understand profile visibility, audience controls and how privacy settings can help protect personal information.

🔑

Password & 2FA

Learn why strong unique passwords and two-factor authentication are important for account security.

🎣

Phishing Awareness

Recognize suspicious links, messages and urgent requests that may attempt to obtain sensitive information.

👤

Fake Profiles

Learn how to identify suspicious profiles and verify unknown accounts before interacting with them.

📍

Oversharing & Location

Understand how sharing locations, routines, travel plans and personal information can create unnecessary risks.

🌐

Digital Footprint

Understand how posts, comments, photos, likes and other online activities can become part of a lasting digital trace.

💬

Cyberbullying

Promote respectful communication and learn how to respond to harassment and harmful online behaviour.

⭐

Digital Reputation

Understand how online information can influence future academic, social and professional opportunities.
